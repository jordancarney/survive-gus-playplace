🎙️ Record the voices

Press ● Record, say the line like the character would, then press ■ Stop. Press ▶ Listen to hear it. Happy with it? Press 💾 Save. Not happy? Just record it again. Quiet bits at the start and end get cut off for you, so don't rush.

Saved lines go to your Downloads folder. When you're done, tell Claude to add the voices to the game (or run python3 dev/add_voices.py).
