HOW TO PLAY

Survive until 6 AM. Watch the cameras from your desk. If you see an animatronic off the stage — or anything weird — go out and check.

Zap them with your taser, then drag them (E) to Parts & Service and chuck them in the junk pile. They're out for the rest of the night.

Fix broken games. Happy kids make happy animatronics, and that makes every night easier.

If the power goes out, fix the fuse box fast. That's when Gus comes out — and nothing stops Gus except the Magnum.

Crouch (C) to hide: nobody can see you except Gus — but you can't zap or drag anything while you're down there.

Get caught and you start the night over.
